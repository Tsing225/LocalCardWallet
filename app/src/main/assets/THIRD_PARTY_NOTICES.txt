   派生说明 / Derivative notes:

     - China prefixes prefer bcbc (1941 entries); other countries come from banks-db
     - Only display name, country, brand color and BIN prefixes are kept
     - Chinese-localized names are preferred where available

Card face artwork
  This application does NOT bundle any bank card face images, brand logos,
  or artwork owned by card issuers. All card faces are drawn procedurally
  in code (Compose Canvas) from a brand color plus a geometric template,
  or supplied by the user from their own photo library.
